# Data Analytics & Business Intelligence Portfolio

Welcome to my Data Analytics and Business Intelligence Portfolio, a collection of practical projects demonstrating my ability to transform raw data into meaningful insights that support informed decision-making.

I am a Data Analyst and Business Intelligence Analyst with an interest in data-driven problem-solving, business performance evaluation, research and analytical reporting. My work focuses on exploring datasets, identifying patterns, uncovering trends and communicating findings in ways that help organisations understand their data and make better decisions.

## Technical Skills

My analytical toolkit includes:

- **Microsoft Excel:** Data cleaning, manipulation, exploratory analysis, pivot tables, advanced formulas and reporting.
- **SQL:** Data extraction, filtering, aggregation, joins and querying relational datasets to answer business questions.
- **Microsoft Power BI:** Interactive dashboards, data modelling, DAX calculations, visualisation and business intelligence reporting.
- **Power Query:** Data transformation, preparation and workflow automation.
- **Data Analysis:** Exploratory data analysis, descriptive statistics, trend identification, performance evaluation and insight generation.

## Portfolio Projects

This repository features practical analytical projects across different business and organisational contexts, including:

1. **Sales Performance Analysis:** Evaluating revenue, sales trends, product performance and business growth opportunities.
2. **Customer Behaviour Analysis:** Exploring purchasing patterns, customer preferences and behavioural trends.
3. **Fraud Detection and Risk Analysis:** Investigating transaction patterns and identifying potential indicators of fraudulent activities.
4. **HR Analytics:** Analysing workforce data, employee distribution, retention and organisational performance.
5. **Business Performance Analysis:** Translating operational and business data into measurable insights and recommendations.

## My Approach

Each project follows a structured analytical process involving problem identification, data understanding, data cleaning, analysis, visualisation, interpretation and evidence-based recommendations. Project documentation will outline the objectives, tools, methodology, findings and practical implications of the analysis.
